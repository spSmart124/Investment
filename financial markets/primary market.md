# Primary Market
The primary market provides the channel for sale of new securities. Primary market provides opportunity to issuers of securities to raise resources to meet their requirements.

## Shares
Shares are an indivisible unit of a company's capital. Primary market issues the securities at face value, or at a discount/premium. These securities may take a variety of forms such as equity, debt etc. They may issue the securities in domestic market or in international market.

## Face Value
The nominal or stated amount (in rupees) assigned to a security by the issuer. It is the original cost of stock shown on the certificate for shares. For bonds, it is the amount paid to the holder at maturity. It is also known as par value or simply par.

For equity shares, the face value usually is a very small amount Rs. 5/, Rs. 10/ etc. It does not have much bearing on the price of the share which may quote Rs. 100/ or Rs. 1000/ in the market or any other value market decides.

For Debt securities, the face value is the amount repaid to the investors when the bond matures. Usually government securities and corporate bonds have a face value of Rs. 100/. The price at which the securities trade will depend on the fluctuations in the interest rate in the economy.

## Issue of Shares
Most companies are started privately by their promoter or promoters. However, promoters' capital and the company borrowings may not be sufficient for setting up or running the business over a long term when the business grows and looks to expand.

Companies invite the public to contribute towards the equity and issue shares to individual investors. This form of issue is also known as Public Issue. Simply stated, a public issue is an offer to the public to subscribe to the share capital of a company. Shares are allotted to applicants as per the prescribed rules and regulations laid down by SEBI.

## Types of Issue
Issues are 3 types.
* Public
* Rights
* Preferential

Here is the sub categorization of Public Issue.
* Initial Public Offering
    * Fresh Issue
    * Offer for Sale
* Follow-on Offering
    * Fresh Issue
    * Offer for Sale
