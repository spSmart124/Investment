# Benefits of Investment
## What is Investment?
The money earned is partly spent and rest is saved for meeting future expenses. Instead of keeping savings idle, you may like to use savings to get returns on it in future. This is called **Investment**.

## Why Invest?
* Earn returns on idle resources
* Generate a specified sum of money for a specific goal in life
* Make provisions for an uncertain future
* Meet the cost of inflation

## Inflation Effect in Investment
Inflation is the rate at which the cost of living increases. It causes money to lose value because it will not buy the same amount of goods or services in the future as it does now or did in the past.

### Example
If there was a 6% inflation rate for the next 20 years a Rs. 100 purchased today would cost 321 in 20 years.

![Effect of Inflation](../resources/effect%20of%20inflation.png)


So, the aim of investments should be to provide a return above the inflation rate to ensure that the investment does not decrease in value.

For example, if annual interest rate is 6% than the investment will need to earn a rate of return to be more than 6% to ensure it increases in value. If the after tax return on your investment is less than the inflation rate than your investment will actually decrease in value. That is they don't buy as much today as it did last year.

## When and How to Invest
The three golden rules for all investors are:
* Invest early
* Invest regularly
* Invest for long term and not short term

## Investment Checklist
Before making any investment, the 12 important steps to follow are:
* Obtain written documents about the investment
* Read and understand the documents
* Verify legitimacy of the investment
* Find its costs and benefits
* Assess risk-return profile
* Know the liquidity and safety aspects
* Ascertain if it is appropriate for your specific goals
* Compare these details with other investment opportunities
* Examine if it fits in with other investments you are considering or have already made
* Deal only through an authorised intermediary
* Seek clarifications about the intermediary and investment
* Explore the options available to you, if something were to go wrong
