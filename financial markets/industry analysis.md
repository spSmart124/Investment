# Industry Analysis
To analyze a company systematically, you must perform
* Industry Analysis
* Corporate Analysis
* Financial Analysis

## Industry Analysis
Companies producing similar products are part of same industry or sector. For example, NHPC, NTPC, Tata Power Company (TPC) belong to power sector/industry. How an industry is faring makes an impact to the company that belongs to this industry. To analyze a company one needs to check
* Government policies
* Future demand of products

Prospect of an industry can change by any alteration in the business environment. For example, a drop in rupee value is a good news for the export industry.

## Corporate Analysis
Corporate Analysis includes checking
* how a company has been faring over past few years
* its operations
* managerial capabilities
* present growth plans
* past performance compared with its competitor

## Financial Analysis
If performance of the industry and the company both seem good then check if company shares are a good buy at the current price. Look at some key parameters like
* Earning per share (EPS)
* P/E ratio
* Current size of equity

This will help arrive at estimated future price. This is termed as financial analysis. For financial analysis understand **Balance Sheet** and **Profit and Loss Account** from Annual report.

### Annual Report
**Annual Report** is a financial statement issued by the company every year. It shows:
* Assets
* Liabilities
* Revenues
* Expenses
* Earnings
* Company's performance at the end of business year
* Profit-wise faring
* Information of interest to shareholders

Abridged annual report of the company is sent to the investors free of cost. A detailed annual report is sent on request. This is the best source of information about a companies financial health.

Which features of an Annual Report should one read carefully?
* One must read an Annual Report with emphasis on the following:
* Director's Report and Chairman's statement which are related to the current and future operational performance of a company.
* Management Discussion and Analysis or MD&A, which talks about the past performance and the future prospects of the company and the industry in which it operates.
* Auditors' Report (including Annexure to the Auditors Report)
* Profit and Loss Account.
* Balance Sheet.
* Notes to accounts attached to the Balance Sheet.

### Balance Sheet
**Balance Sheet** of a company shows the financial position of the company at a particular point of time. According to companies act 2013, the balance sheet of a company should be either in **Account Form** or **Report Form**.

The **Profit and Loss Statment** or **Income Statement** shows the financial performance of the company over a period of time. It indicates revenue and expenses during that particular period. This period or account period is April to March. The **Accounting Report** summarizes the revenue items, the expense items and the difference between them which is the net income for an accounting period.

## Share Capital
When a company starts operation it's owners called shareholders contribute funds called **Share Capital**. Shareholders being owners of the company share part of the profit as dividends. Share capital has been further divided into
* Equity Capital
* Preference Capital

Equity capital does not have a fixed rate of dividend. Preference capital represents contribution by preference shareholders and has a fixed rate of dividend. After distributing dividend a part of the profit is retained by the company for meeting fund requirements for future. The retained profits accumulated for the years are called reserve or surplus which are shareholders property.
